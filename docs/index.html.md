## Questions

## Is there an app to practice cribbage?

Yes. Cribbage Trainer is a free iPhone and iPad app that drills counting the hand, choosing the discard and pegging to 31 in five-minute sessions. Each answer comes with the coach's reasoning.

## Is Cribbage Trainer a full cribbage game?

No. It is a practice app, not a table. There are no opponents or bots, so you work on your counting, discards and pegging without playing a whole game.

## How do I get better at counting cribbage hands?

Practice reading five cards for fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and nobs until the pattern jumps out. The Scoring Room and Hand reading drills in Cribbage Trainer do exactly that and explain every score.

## How do I choose what to discard to the crib?

Keep the best scoring potential for your own hand, then weigh what the two cards you throw give the crib, which depends on whether it is yours or your opponent's. The Discard Room lets you work six-card deals and compare your choice with the coach's pick and the crib tradeoff.

## Can I practice cribbage pegging on my phone?

Yes. The Pegging Room covers fifteens, pairs, runs, go, 31, last card and safe totals while the board total stays in view.

## Is Cribbage Trainer free, and does it need an account?

The download is free, with four practice rooms, flashcards and streaks included, and there is no account or sign-up. An optional Cribbage+ upgrade adds extra practice sets and the Master Tables, and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Every drill works with no network connection, and your progress stays on your device.
