## Common questions

**How do I restore Cribbage+?**
Open Settings in the app
and choose Restore Purchases. Apple must be signed in to the account that
made the purchase.

**How do I cancel?**
Open iPhone Settings, tap your
name, tap Subscriptions, then choose Cribbage Trainer.

**Is this a full cribbage game?**
No. Cribbage Trainer is a
practice companion for counting, pegging, discarding, and table decisions.

**Why does my score differ from another table?**
House
rules and counting conventions vary. Each explanation states the
assumptions used by the practice item.
