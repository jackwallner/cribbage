Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Cribbity is designed to be useful without an account and to keep your practice history on your device.

Information stored on your device

Cribbity stores practice progress, streaks, preferences, review history, and onboarding choices locally on your device. This information is not uploaded by Cribbity. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to the normal behavior of your device backups.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. Cribbity uses RevenueCat to read and manage subscription status. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app identifier and purchase or entitlement information. It does not receive your name or email from Cribbity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that message. We use it to answer your question and do not sell it.

What Cribbity does not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data.
  • No sale of personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to jackwallner+m@gmail.com.
