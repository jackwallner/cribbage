Cribbage Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Cribbage Trainer is designed to work without an account. Practice progress, streaks, preferences, review history, and onboarding choices are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by the app.

Purchases and support

Apple processes Cribbage+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies subscription status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical context. It does not receive your practice content, name, or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. If you email support, we receive the information you include and use it to answer you.

What we do not collect

No account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, tracking pixel, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local practice data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Support messages are retained only as needed to answer and document the request. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies.

Children and changes

Cribbage Trainer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
