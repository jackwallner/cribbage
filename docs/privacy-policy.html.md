## Cribbage Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Cribbage Trainer is designed to work without an account. Practice
progress, streaks, preferences, review history, and onboarding choices
are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by the app.

## Purchases and support

Apple processes Cribbage+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies subscription
status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and
entitlement information, and limited technical context. It does not
receive your practice content, name, or email. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). If you email support, we receive the information you include and use
it to answer you.

## Ad attribution

If you install Cribbage Trainer from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's
AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to
the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you
across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for
themselves.

## What we do not collect

No account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, tracking pixel,
location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. We do not sell
personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local practice data remains until you delete it or remove the app.
Support messages are retained only as needed to answer and document the
request. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their
policies.

## Children and changes

Cribbage Trainer is not directed to children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this
policy when the app or its services change.
