CRIBBITY

Practice the hand. Play the table.

Calm, focused drills for counting, pegging, discarding, and the small decisions that make a cribbage hand easier to read.

Cribbity is an independent cribbage practice companion. There are no opponents, no account, and no pressure. Open a room, take a few reps, and bring the explanation back to your next game.

Four free practice rooms

  • The Card RoomLearn the deck, card values, starter, crib, and race to 121.
  • The Scoring RoomRecognize fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes, nobs, and count order.
  • The Discard RoomChoose two cards for the crib and understand the tradeoff.
  • The Pegging RoomPractice 15s, 31, pairs, runs, go, last card, and running totals.

Cribbity+

Cribbity+ adds generated practice, spaced review, a timed challenge, extra sets in every beginner room, and The Master Tables for advanced decisions. The free rooms remain free.

New practice is added as the library grows.
