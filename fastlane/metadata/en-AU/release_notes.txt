A smarter rhythm for cribbage night.

Cribbage Minute is a new daily challenge. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two hand reads, one discard decision, and two pegging calls. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual cribbage night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Cribbage Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

Cribbage Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Cribbage+.
