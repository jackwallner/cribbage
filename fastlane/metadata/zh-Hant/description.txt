Practice standard-deck cribbage in five-minute drills: count hands, read the pegging board, choose strong discards, and learn the reason behind every answer.

Cribbage Trainer is a focused practice companion for players learning the game or sharpening decisions between table sessions. No opponents, no pressure, and no account required. Just quick interactive reps that build confidence before the next deal.

FOUR FREE PRACTICE ROOMS

THE CARD ROOM
Learn the standard deck, card values, fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes, his nobs, the starter, the crib, and the race to 121.

THE SCORING ROOM
See a hand, identify its scoring shapes, and build a reliable order for counting fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes, nobs, and the crib.

THE DISCARD ROOM
Study the tradeoff between your hand and the dealer's crib. Choose two cards to discard, then compare your decision with the coach's reasoning.

THE PEGGING ROOM
Practice running totals, 15s, 31, pairs, runs, go, last card, and the small decisions that turn a pegging sequence into points.
