Cribbage Trainer is a free app for practicing cribbage between games. It turns counting, pegging and discarding into short drills you can finish over a cup of coffee, and every answer comes with the reasoning behind it.

There is no opponent to keep up with and no account to create. Open a room, take a few reps, and carry the habit to your next game.

FOUR ROOMS, FREE FOR GOOD

The Card Room
Meet the standard deck the way cribbage sees it: card values, fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes, his nobs, the starter card, the crib and the race to 121.

The Scoring Room
Look at a five-card hand and name the shape worth counting first. Build a steady counting order so nothing gets left on the table.

The Discard Room
Six cards dealt, two must go. Pick the pair you would lay away, then see what the coach would throw and why, from both the dealer's and the pone's side.

The Pegging Room
Work through running totals, fifteen and thirty-one, pairs and runs in play, go and last card, and the small choices that win pegging points.

HOW PRACTICE FEELS
Flashcards flip with a tap and swipe away once you know them. Choice questions grade the moment you answer and hold the explanation on screen until you are ready to move on. Discard deals let you pick two cards and compare. A daily streak keeps the habit going.

CRIBBAGE+ (OPTIONAL)
The four rooms stay free. Cribbage+ adds practice that does not run out:
- Endless Practice deals brand new hands for counting and pegging, so you never see the same question twice
- Fix My Mistakes brings back what you missed until it sticks
- Timed Challenge gives you ninety seconds and a best score to beat
- Cribbage Minute is the same five-question challenge for every member each day
- Game Night Prep builds a five-minute warm-up around your weak spots
- Extra practice sets in every room, plus the Master Tables for advanced discards, defense and expert counting

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your practice history stays on your device.
