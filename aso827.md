## Executive directive

Keep the `Cribbage Trainer` brand, current App Store title, and current subtitle. The visible fields already produce the app's best proven search compounds: #2 for `cribbage trainer` and #6 for `cribbage counting`. Do not disturb them without download evidence.

Add hand, learn, points, and other literal practice concepts while preserving muggins and pegboard long tails. The target is a 98-byte field with no exact visible-field repetition.

Replace the screenshot package even though it has six accepted iPhone and six accepted iPad assets. The current creative uses an eyebrow plus headline, fails the one-header contract, and several literal screens do not prove their outer claims. Use an eight-frame product story with hand-value uncertainty, a real decision, and visible explanation first.

Regional recommendations are provisional. The supplied analytics bundle has no Cribbage entry, so there is no honest territory-level funnel. Prioritize Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, and France based on current search evidence, language fit, product fit, and existing localization coverage, then replace that order when ASC exports become available.

## Product truth and claim boundaries

Supported by current source and metadata:

- Five-minute mixed practice sessions
- Card, Scoring, Discard, and Pegging free rooms
- Master Tables paid room
- Counting, scoring patterns, discard choices, pegging judgments, and explanations
- Generated hands through Endless Practice
- Fix My Mistakes spaced review
- Timed Challenge
- Cribbage Minute
- Game Night Prep
- Local progress, streaks, and missed-item storage
- No opponents and no account requirement
- Native iPhone and iPad support

Avoid or qualify:

- `No pressure` conflicts with a 90-second Timed Challenge if presented as an app-wide absolute. Use `practice at your pace` for untimed rooms.
- `Five minutes` is an intended session length, not a guarantee for every flow.
- `Never run out` belongs only on the generated Endless Practice frame.
- `Turn misses into better play` describes a review mechanism, not guaranteed long-term skill.
- Cribbage has house-rule variations. Preserve the current qualifier.
- Do not imply the app deals a complete game against opponents.
